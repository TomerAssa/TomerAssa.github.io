01 — Frontend / Design

A map-driven travel journal

02 — Backend / Agents WIP

A league of job-hunting agents

A multi-agent pipeline that finds roles in the Israeli startup ecosystem: discovers careers/ATS pages via SERP, extracts open positions, matches them against your network, and tailors a CV per role. Persisted in SQLite; LLM runs locally by default.
